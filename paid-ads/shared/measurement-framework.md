# Paid Ads Measurement Framework

Use comparable units before comparing channels or periods.

| Metric | Definition | Required qualifier |
|---|---|---|
| CPA | Spend divided by the named, qualified conversion count | Conversion definition and attribution window |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue divided by spend | Attribution source/window and whether revenue is gross or net |
| ACoS | Spend divided by attributed ad revenue | Margin or break-even ACoS when judging it |
| CTR | Link clicks divided by impressions | Link clicks, not all clicks, and placement/channel |
| Frequency | Impressions divided by reached people | Date window and audience segment |
| Pacing | Spend to date divided by elapsed-period expected spend | Budget period, currency, and days elapsed |

Compare complete equivalent windows (for example, the last seven completed days against the seven before). Do not average platform CPAs or ROAS values without a spend-weighted calculation and a stated definition; in-platform attribution remains non-comparable unless the conversion and window match.

For a recommendation, attach an observable hypothesis: the named change, target metric, guardrail, review date, and the outcome that would reverse the decision.
